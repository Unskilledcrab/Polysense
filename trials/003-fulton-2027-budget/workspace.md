## Layer 2: The decision

**What's being decided:** How much Fulton County will spend in 2027, and on what. [P-005, P-042] The full Board of Commissioners decides. [P-014] Once submitted, the Budget Commission's proposal serves as the operating budget until the Board adopts one. [P-005]

**Not published yet:** On Nov 8, the county's budget page had no 2027 proposal, and its Public Notices page had no budget hearing date. {analysis: P-045 and P-042, captured Nov 8, 2026} The 2027 meeting schedule is posted only as a proposal. [P-041, P-040]

**What's different this year:**

1. A four-member Budget Commission helps develop the draft; since 2018, the county manager had done it alone. [P-005, P-008, P-003]
2. Its members are the county manager, the chief financial officer, Commissioner Bob Ellis (north) and Vice Chair Khadijah Abdur-Rahman (south). [P-008, P-021]
3. It works by agreement; if it can't agree, the county manager decides how to present the draft. [P-014]
4. Only the full Board can adopt or change the budget. [P-014]
5. Departments were asked to keep requests for extra money within 5% of their current funding. [P-029, P-077] That is how Commissioner Barrett's resolution and a FOX 5 report describe the limit. [P-029, P-077, P-080] We found no written instruction, and no source we reviewed disputes that the limit exists. {analysis: see README, D4}

### How the budget is drafted

- From 1953 until 2018, a budget commission drafted Fulton's budget. [P-002, P-003] Its members were the Board chairman, the county manager and the chief financial officer. [P-002] In 2018, the Board gave that job to the county manager alone. [P-003] Resolution 26-0460, adopted Aug 5, 2026, brought a commission back "in a modified form." [P-004, P-017] Commissioner Ellis and Vice Chair Abdur-Rahman sponsored it. [P-016, P-017]
- The county manager and chief financial officer still prepare the budget documents and ask departments for their budget proposals. [P-012]
- The two commissioner members are chosen before each budget cycle at a noticed Board meeting. [P-009] One comes from districts 1 to 3, and one from districts 4 to 6. [P-008] A member must have served a year, and must not have a term ending in the year the budget is drafted. [P-010] The rules have a fallback if no one in a group is eligible. [P-010] An eligible vice chair serves automatically. [P-011] For the 2027 budget, Ellis was the only eligible member from districts 1 to 3. [P-021]
- The two commissioners must ask all commissioners for their budget priorities. [P-013] They take part in reviewing departments' requests, and they report to the full Board. [P-013]

- **Who benefits:** If the Board adds money, the offices it funds gain; if it cuts, taxpayers and the county's savings gain. {analysis: both directions are allowed (P-047)}
- **Who pays:** If the Board adds money, taxpayers, other programs or savings pay; if it cuts, the offices cut pay. {analysis} The 2026 plan spends $73,637,614 more than it takes in. [P-054]
