### How the budget is drafted

- From 1953 until 2018, a budget commission drafted Fulton's budget. [P-002, P-003] Its members were the Board chairman, the county manager and the chief financial officer. [P-002] In 2018, the Board gave that job to the county manager alone. [P-003] Resolution 26-0460, adopted Aug 5, 2026, brought a commission back "in a modified form." [P-004, P-017] Commissioner Ellis and Vice Chair Abdur-Rahman sponsored it. [P-016, P-017]
- The new commission's members are the county manager, the chief financial officer, Commissioner Bob Ellis (north) and Vice Chair Khadijah Abdur-Rahman (south). [P-008, P-021] The county manager and chief financial officer still prepare the budget documents and ask departments for their budget proposals. [P-012] The commission works by agreement; if it can't agree, the county manager decides how to present the draft. [P-014]
- The two commissioner members are chosen before each budget cycle at a publicly announced Board meeting. [P-009] One comes from districts 1 to 3, and one from districts 4 to 6. [P-008] To be eligible, a member must have served a year, and must not have a term ending in the year the budget is drafted. [P-010] The rules have a fallback if no one in a group is eligible. [P-010] An eligible vice chair serves automatically. [P-011] For the 2027 budget, Ellis was the only eligible member from districts 1 to 3. [P-021]
- The two commissioners must ask all commissioners for their budget priorities. [P-013] They take part in reviewing departments' requests, and they report to the full Board. [P-013]
- The county code says the proposal "will serve, as presented, as the operating budget until a final budget is adopted." [P-005] The operating budget is the spending plan the county runs on. {analysis: plain-language definition} The proposal covers 2027, so this applies only to 2027 spending before the Board adopts a budget. {analysis: P-005 says the proposal governs spending "for the following year," and adoption is due by the second regular meeting in January}
