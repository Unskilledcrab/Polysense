# Owner feedback on the experience: Trial 003 (Fulton County 2027 budget)

Facts and fairness are checked by the [verification protocol](../../docs/process/verification-protocol.md), with
no human in the loop. **This form is only about the experience:** is it easy to use, easy to find things, and
above all easy to understand?

Open the prototype on your phone if you can. This trial has not been rendered into `prototypes/` yet; the
coordinating session can build it with `trials/tools/render_prototype.py` (slug `fulton-budget-2027`, settings in
[page.yaml](page.yaml)). A test render worked alongside Trial 001.

## Five questions

1. After 30 seconds on the Fulton County budget page, could you say what is being decided and when?
2. Was it obvious what *you* could do about it, and by when?
3. Did any part feel like it was taking a side? Which words?
4. Did the source chips and labels make you trust it more, or just add clutter?
5. On your phone, was anything hard to tap, read or find?
