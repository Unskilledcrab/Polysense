# Owner feedback on the experience: Trial 002 (PSC Docket 57171)

Facts and fairness are checked by the [verification protocol](verification.md), with no human in the
loop. **This form is only about the experience:** is it easy to use, easy to find things, and above all
easy to understand?

Open the prototype on your phone if you can. It has not been rendered or published for this trial yet; the coordinating
session can build it with `trials/tools/render_prototype.py` (this trial's `page.yaml` uses the slug `psc-57171`).
You can also leave comments directly on the published page once it exists.

## Five questions

1. After 30 seconds on the PSC 57171 page, could you say what is being decided and when?
2. Was it obvious what *you* could do about it, and by when?
3. Did any part feel like it was taking a side? Which words?
4. Did the source chips and labels make you trust it more, or just add clutter?
5. On your phone, was anything hard to tap, read or find?
