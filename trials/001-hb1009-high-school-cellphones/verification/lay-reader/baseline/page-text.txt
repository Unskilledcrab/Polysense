The 30-second version

WHAT IT DOES

Public high school students won't be allowed to use phones or similar devices during the school day, including between classes.Law
Schools must start no later than July 1, 2027.Law
Students may use a device as their health or disability plan explicitly requires.Law

WHO IT AFFECTS

Public high school students, their families, and the school staff who carry out the rules.Law

WHAT'S NEXT

The law takes effect Dec 31, 2026.Law
Each district sets details, like where phones are kept.Law
You can tell your school district how you want the rules run, or tell your state lawmakers whether to keep or change the law.Our framing

What you can do

The law takes effect.Law

BY JAN 1 EACH YEAR

Every district must adopt its device policy, revising it as needed, now covering kindergarten through 12th grade.Law
The first such deadline under the new law is Jan 1, 2027.Our analysis

BY JUL 1, 2027

High schools must enforce the ban, at the latest.Law

At least one school planned to start sooner: on June 1, 2026, Cairo High School said it would begin in the 2026-2027 school year.Cairo High
