House Bill 1009 · 2026 · Act 395

Georgia lawmakers ban school-day phone use in public high schools by July 1, 2027
Signed into law May 5, 2026
Takes effect Dec 31, 2026
High schools start by July 1, 2027
Reader-first update; checks pending

House Bill (HB) 1009 will bar public high school students from using phones and similar devices during the school day, unless a health or disability plan explicitly requires one. Cairo High Law

May matter to you:

If you're a student at a Georgia public high school: By July 1, 2027, at the latest, you can't use phones or similar devices at any point in the school day, with some exceptions. Law

If your child attends a Georgia public high school: Starting no later than July 1, 2027, you'll need to contact the school to reach your child during the school day. Law

If you work at a Georgia public high school: By July 1, 2027, at the latest, your school must enforce the phone ban, which may include assigning staff and checking phone storage. Law

If your child is in kindergarten through 8th grade at a Georgia public school: A 2025 law already bars these students from using phones and similar devices during the school day. Law Governor

The 30-second version

WHAT IT DOES

House Bill (HB) 1009 will bar public high school students from using phones and similar devices during the school day, unless a health or disability plan explicitly requires one. Cairo High Law
Georgia lawmakers and the governor approved the law. Law Bill record

WHO IT AFFECTS

Public high school students, their families and school staff. Law

WHAT'S NEXT

High schools must start the ban by July 1, 2027, and districts set the details. Law
You can ask your district how it will run the rules, or tell lawmakers whether to keep or change the law. Our note
What you can do ↓
What it means for you

If you're a student at a Georgia public high school: By July 1, 2027, at the latest, you can't use phones or similar devices at any point in the school day, with some exceptions. Law Breaking the rule brings consequences that grow each time, such as warnings or taking the device. Law Your school may start sooner. Our analysis

If your child attends a Georgia public high school: Starting no later than July 1, 2027, you'll need to contact the school to reach your child during the school day. Law All emergency messages will go through the school's own systems. Law The law sets aside no money and doesn't require families to pay for anything. Our analysis

If you work at a Georgia public high school: By July 1, 2027, at the latest, your school must enforce the phone ban, which may include assigning staff and checking phone storage. Law The law sets aside no money for these duties. Our analysis The Georgia Department of Education must give schools guidance and technical support. Law

If your child is in kindergarten through 8th grade at a Georgia public school: A 2025 law already bars these students from using phones and similar devices during the school day. Law Governor HB 1009 leaves that rule as it was. Our analysis The new law makes districts and schools adopt or update their policies by January 1 every year, not just once. Law

THE GOVERNOR

Gov. Brian Kemp signed HB 1009 into law on May 5, 2026. Bill record Cairo High
Our sources give no phone number, email or meeting for the governor's office. Our note

EACH SCHOOL DISTRICT AND PUBLIC SCHOOL

Each district and public school sets the details in a policy, which it must adopt, and revise as needed, by January 1 each year. Law
The law calls a district a "local school system." Law
The rules must appear in student and family handbooks, codes of conduct and school websites. Law
The law encourages districts and schools to hold family workshops, question-and-answer sessions and town halls. Law
Our sources give no phone numbers or emails for districts. Our note
