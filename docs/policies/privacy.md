# Privacy (draft)

**We personalize by district, not by profiling you.**

## What we collect, and what we don't

| Data | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Your address** | Used once to find your districts, then **discarded**. Never stored, logged or sent to analytics |
| **Your districts** | Saved **only in your browser** (local storage) and in the page address you choose to bookmark (e.g. `/my/h054-s039/`). Districts cover tens of thousands of people |
| **Topics you filter by** | Part of the page address (URL parameters). Not stored on our side |
| **Accounts** | None during the pilot |
| **Challenges** | Your report is public, minus personal details. Your optional email is used only to reply and is **deleted when the challenge closes** |
| **Usage analytics** | Aggregate, cookieless and privacy-preserving only. No third-party trackers or ad pixels |
| **Political views** | **Never** collected, inferred or profiled |

## How lookups work

Your address is sent once to the US Census Geocoder to get map coordinates. We then find your districts
on our own maps. Neither the address nor the coordinates are kept, and our error reporting removes
addresses automatically.

If the geocoder can't find your address, you can choose your districts from a list instead.

## Your choices

- Clear your saved districts at any time.
- The site works without saving anything, using the statewide view.
