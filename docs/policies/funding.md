# Funding and independence (draft)

## Current funding

None. The project is volunteer-built.

## Rules we will follow

1. **Publish every funder and amount** above a small threshold, updated quarterly.
2. **Cap any single funder at about 20%** of annual revenue. Concentrated funders have abruptly
   withdrawn from comparable projects (Google from Full Fact, Meta from US fact-checking).
3. **No money** from political parties, candidates, campaigns, PACs, or groups whose main purpose is
   lobbying on bills we cover.
4. **Funders get no say** over coverage, conclusions or corrections. Grant agreements must say so.
5. **Seek funders from across the political spectrum,** and show the mix.
6. **Earned revenue** (data licensing, partnerships) must not create incentives to slant or
   sensationalize. No advertising and no engagement-driven revenue.

## Structure path

1. **Now:** volunteer project.
2. **Before public beta:** join a **fiscal sponsor** (Model A: the sponsor holds the funds and the legal
   responsibility). Get media liability insurance and counsel review of the policies.
3. **After a successful pilot:** consider a standalone 501(c)(3).

## Where to look for funding

Civic-tech and journalism foundations (Knight, Democracy Fund, Hewlett, Arnold Ventures, Craig Newmark
Philanthropies, Rita Allen), Georgia community foundations, and small-dollar supporters. See
[research/landscape.md](../research/landscape.md#funding-and-sustainability).
